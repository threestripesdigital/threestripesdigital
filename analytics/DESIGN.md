# Rank Boost Command Center

Private operational dashboard. Calm ivory and white surfaces, forest green identity, mint learning banner, amber OK signals, red problem signals. System sans serif with tabular numerals. Status always includes a word so color is supplemental.

## Responsive Behaviour

Desktop sidebar at 240px, with a 205px compact variant. At 760px and below, navigation becomes a horizontal row above the content. KPI cards use two columns on mobile, signal cards use one. Data tables scroll within their panels without widening the page. Form controls have visible focus and at least 42px height, mobile navigation 44px. No animation is required. Reduced-motion preference is respected. Verify at desktop and mobile widths.

Overview prioritizes booked, showed and qualified calls. Pending outcomes are counted beside those KPIs. Incomplete rate badges say Provisional; unset benchmarks say No target. Sales qualification and keyword eligibility are separate columns. Manual outcome fields retain the existing two-column desktop and single-column mobile form layout.

When custom browser collection is disabled, attributed funnel KPIs say Unavailable and related metric cards show No data. Direct GA4 reports use a separate overview block, scoped to the law firm funnel across all traffic. Missing results are never displayed as observed zero visits. Connections distinguish successful reporting access, absent data, and missing provider credentials. No tracking script is added to the landing page by this dashboard.

Direct Wistia video reporting sits beneath GA4, with plays, unique plays, unique player loads, play rate, average engagement and watched seconds. Its all-traffic and prelaunch scope is visible, along with reporting dates, import time and stale/error states. It does not populate the old paid-retention benchmark.

Provider results occupy the existing metric grids. Meta website actions sit with performance signals. Wistia fills the VSL play-rate card plus adjacent volume, engagement and watch-time cards; GA4 visits sit in the funnel grid. Each provider card includes source and traffic scope. Average engagement never substitutes for 50% retention. Detail dialogs use the same card values. No loose statistics text block is appended.

Provider-backed strategy metrics retain their configured thresholds. Play rate compares Wistia all-traffic values to the supplied 40% ideal and 25% bad boundary, with the scope visible. Metric values, badges and bands reflect the computed status. Cards display ideal, OK range and bad boundary, including when data is missing. Counts and other metrics without supplied targets remain unrated.

Campaigns & creatives includes a live placement evaluation and Meta-rendered previews in desktop and mobile screen frames. Artwork is also shown without distortion with its exact pixel dimensions. The evaluation states Passed or Not ready in text. Missing enhancement settings, unsupported placements, wrong dimensions, and stale visual reviews fail closed. Users inspect each enabled preview and explicitly confirm legibility, cropping and safe areas. A review binds to a fingerprint of the creative, assets and targeting, so configuration changes invalidate it. The dashboard remains unable to activate ads or change spend.

Provider availability is evaluated per metric. Disabling custom collection does not hide measured provider values or Calendly-attributed call outcomes. Cost cards cover landing page views, VSL plays and completed forms. VSL ad rows show their source and capture window; historical spend is never divided by partial PostHog plays. Session recordings open in the dedicated private Rank Boost PostHog project.
