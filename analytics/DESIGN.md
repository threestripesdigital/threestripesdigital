# Rank Boost Command Center

Private operational dashboard. Calm ivory and white surfaces, forest green identity, mint learning banner, amber OK signals, red problem signals. System sans serif with tabular numerals. Status always includes a word so color is supplemental.

## Responsive Behaviour

Desktop sidebar at 240px, with a 205px compact variant. At 760px and below, navigation becomes a horizontal row above the content. KPI cards use two columns on mobile, signal cards use one. Data tables scroll within their panels without widening the page. Form controls have visible focus and at least 42px height, mobile navigation 44px. No animation is required. Reduced-motion preference is respected. Verify at desktop and mobile widths.

Overview prioritizes booked, showed and qualified calls. Pending outcomes are counted beside those KPIs. Incomplete rate badges say Provisional; unset benchmarks say No target. Sales qualification and keyword eligibility are separate columns. Manual outcome fields retain the existing two-column desktop and single-column mobile form layout.
