FAQ

Straight answers before you apply

Why is this free? It seems too good to be true.

The initial test is how we demonstrate our approach before discussing paid ongoing work. If your firm qualifies, the test itself is free and requires no credit card or site access. Rankings are controlled by search engines, so movement and timing are not guaranteed.

Is this bot traffic or some black-hat trick?

No. No bots, no click farms, no doorway pages, and we never touch your site (no logins, no access, ever). It’s a focused organic push on pages you already own, and the movement shows up in your own rank tracker and on Google itself, where you can verify it.

How fast will I see movement?

Some past tests showed movement in about 48 hours, but timing and results vary. We qualify first and review the measured before-and-after rather than promise an outcome.

How long does the boost last?

We agree on a review window before the test runs. The appropriate window varies by keyword and market, and we compare the before-and-after together.

What does it cost?

Nothing. The organic rank boost is completely free if you qualify, no credit card, no contract. Everything else gets discussed on the call, after you’ve seen your rankings move.

Will this help me show up in AI search (ChatGPT, etc.)?

Organic visibility may support broader discovery, but AI recommendation systems are separate and no placement is guaranteed. One past client reported reaching #2 in a ChatGPT recommendation during their campaign; that example does not predict future results.

Is this local pack / Google Maps ranking?

No. The free boost test focuses on organic keyword / SERP positions, roughly 11–50. Local pack / Maps is a different kind of work and is not included.

Who is this for, and who should skip it?

Fit: law firm decision-makers with money keywords stuck around positions 11–50 who want to see real movement on their own rankings before making any decisions.

Skip: if you only want map pack from a 48-hour boost, need “AI SEO” buzzwords more than position changes, or want us to also run your direct competitor in the same niche and city.

Do you take multiple firms in the same niche and city?

No. One client per niche per city. Exclusivity is part of the offer.
